GOLFGAME

Top-down minigolf with fading gravity. Up to 10 golfers gather in the lobby, pick an 8- or 16-hole course, and then play the same randomly generated holes at the same time. Every shot starts with planets at full pull, fading to zero as the ball covers half the distance it would have rolled without them. Sink the cup, beat your rivals. Server-authoritative physics on SpacetimeDB @ 20 Hz.
