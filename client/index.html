GOLFGAME

Top-down minigolf with fading gravity. Every shot starts with planets at full pull, which fades to zero as the ball covers half the distance it would have rolled without them — so gravity bends your drive, but never your putt. Server-authoritative physics on SpacetimeDB @ 20 Hz.
